Inside a Council

Your decision stays yours.

The other five women are not there to tell you what to do.

01

Ask questions

Get underneath the decision rather than rushing to an answer.

02

Challenge assumptions

Surface what may be shaping the decision without being examined.

03

Introduce perspective

Bring experience and context from outside your immediate professional world.

04

Stress-test the thinking

Examine the options while the decision is still in play.

Edition One · Founding US pilot

Three Councils.
Six women in each.

Placement is by application and Placement Conversation rather than open enrollment.

For women making a significant professional change.

Changing roles or industries, redundancy, returning after time away, leaving an established path or starting a new professional chapter.

For women thinking seriously about what comes next.

Greater responsibility, visibility, influence or working out the next step when progression is no longer obvious.

For women reconsidering the shape of their professional life.

Changing priorities, recovering from an unsustainable role or questioning whether the version of success you were pursuing still fits.

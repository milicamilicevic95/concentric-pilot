Who it is for

Something consequential is in play.

Concentric is for professionally established women navigating an important professional decision or transition.

  • Considering a move.
  • Re-entering.
  • Leaving something established.
  • Taking on something bigger.
  • Reconsidering what comes next.
  • Or knowing something needs to change before knowing exactly what.

You do not need to arrive with the answer.

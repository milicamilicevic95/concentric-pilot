Why Concentric

Can someone be professionally well-connected and still lack the right people for the conversations that matter?

Concentric began with research into that question.

Inside a Council, members bring decisions, questions and tensions while they are still in play. The other five women are not there to tell them what to do. They ask questions, challenge assumptions, introduce different perspectives and help stress-test the thinking.

The point is not consensus.
It is better thinking.

The Evidence Base

Concentric began with research.

Our first US study asked 67 women about the professional challenges they were navigating, the support available to them and what they felt was missing.

Four themes emerged across the responses.

Direction uncertainty

Women questioning whether their current professional path was still the right one. The report describes uncertainty as a recurring theme rather than evidence of professional failure.

Why Concentric exists

Can you be well-connected and still lack the right people for a particular conversation?

Concentric began with research involving 67 women across the United States.

Many already had friends, colleagues, mentors and professional networks. What they were navigating still raised questions about who they could talk to while a decision was unresolved.

Concentric is testing that question.

Read the research

Inside a Council

Six women. One Council.

Members bring decisions, questions and tensions while they are still in play. The other five women ask questions, challenge assumptions, introduce different perspectives and help stress-test the thinking.

The point is not consensus. It is better thinking.

How Councils are composed

Shared context. Useful difference.

Women are placed around what they are navigating, not simply what they do.

Each Council has enough shared context for members to understand one another and enough difference to bring perspectives the others may not already have.

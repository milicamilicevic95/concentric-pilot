Research 01

What 67 Women Told Us.

A research report on the professional experiences of women navigating significant professional moments in the United States.

Open the full report
67women across the United States

Four themes

Direction uncertainty

Questioning whether the current path is still the right one.

Professional isolation

Managing professional difficulty largely alone.

Access gaps

Missing relationships, sponsorship or opportunity despite professional contacts.

Professional recalibration

Reconsidering work after burnout, depletion or an unsustainable role.

The study was exploratory and the sample was self-selected. It was not designed to represent all professional women in the United States.

The question underneath the research

Can someone be professionally well-connected and still lack the right people for the conversations that matter?

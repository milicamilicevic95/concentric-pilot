About Concentric

Curated peer advisory Councils for professionally established women.

Concentric creates small, deliberately composed Councils for women navigating significant professional decisions.

The model

Six women. 90 minutes a month.

Members bring decisions, questions and tensions while they are still in play.

The other five women ask questions, challenge assumptions, introduce different perspectives and help stress-test the thinking.

The Council does not decide for you. The facilitator does not coach you.

The point is better thinking before the answer is settled.

Why it exists

Concentric began with research.

Our first US study asked 67 women about the professional challenges they were navigating, the support available to them and what they felt was missing.

Across the responses, four themes emerged: direction uncertainty, professional isolation, access gaps and professional recalibration.

The question underneath them was larger: can someone be professionally well-connected and still lack the right people for the conversations that matter?

Concentric is testing that question.

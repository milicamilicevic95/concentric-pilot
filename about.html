About Concentric

The right people for the conversations that matter.

Concentric creates curated peer advisory Councils for professionally established women navigating significant professional decisions.

The model

Six women. 90 minutes a month.

Each Council is deliberately composed around what its members are navigating — with enough shared context to understand one another and enough difference to bring useful perspective.

Members bring decisions, questions and tensions while they are still in play. The other five women ask questions, challenge assumptions, introduce different perspectives and help stress-test the thinking.

The point is not consensus. It is better thinking.

Where it began

Concentric began with research.

Our first US study asked 67 women about the professional challenges they were navigating, the support available to them and what they felt was missing.

Across the responses, four themes emerged: direction uncertainty, professional isolation, access gaps and professional recalibration.

The question underneath them was larger: Can someone be professionally well-connected and still lack the right people for the conversations that matter?

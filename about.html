About Concentric

Built to make one kind of conversation more useful.

Concentric creates curated peer advisory Councils for professionally established women navigating significant professional decisions.

Why Concentric exists

The question came before the company.

Our first US study asked 67 women about the professional challenges they were navigating, the support available to them and what they felt was missing.

The question underneath the responses was larger: can someone be professionally well-connected and still lack the right people for the conversations that matter?

Concentric is testing that question through deliberately composed peer Councils.

The team

Founder, Concentric

Mila founded Concentric following the original research that led to the model.

Dr. Melonie Boone

Council Facilitator

Melonie facilitates Concentric’s founding Councils. Her role is to provide structure and timekeeping so the conversation stays focused and useful. She is not there to coach the group or direct members toward an answer.

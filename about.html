About Concentric

A better room for unfinished professional thinking.

Concentric creates deliberately composed peer Councils for professionally established women navigating important professional decisions and transitions.

What we do

Six women. 90 minutes. Once a month.

Members bring professional decisions, questions and situations they are currently working through.

The other women ask questions, share relevant experience, offer different perspectives and challenge assumptions.

A neutral facilitator provides structure and timekeeping. She does not coach the group or tell members what to do.

The decision remains yours.

Why it exists

The idea came from research.

Our first US research study involved 67 women navigating significant professional moments.

Many already had friends, colleagues, mentors and professional networks.

The question that followed was simple: can someone be professionally well-connected and still lack the right people for a particular conversation?

Concentric was created to test that question.

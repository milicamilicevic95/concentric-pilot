About Concentric

Peer advisory Councils for professionally established women.

Concentric brings together six women for a 90-minute online conversation once a month.

Members bring professional decisions, questions and situations they are currently working through.

The other women ask questions, share relevant experience, offer different perspectives and challenge assumptions.

You make your own decisions.

Why we started

The idea came from research.

Our first US research study involved 67 women navigating significant professional moments.

We heard about women questioning their career direction, managing difficult situations alone, reconsidering work after burnout and struggling to access some of the relationships or opportunities they needed.

Many already had friends, colleagues, mentors and professional networks.

The question that followed was:

Can someone be professionally well-connected and still lack the right people for a particular conversation?

Concentric was created to test that question.

How we got here

From research to a live pilot.

Concentric began with original US research.

We then conducted Placement Conversations and sought perspectives from academics, practitioners and senior women while developing the model.

That work informed Edition One.

The first Council has now met, and we are continuing to build the founding Councils.

Edition One is a pilot.

We will use what happens during it to decide what Concentric should become next.

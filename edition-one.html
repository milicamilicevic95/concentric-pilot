The three Councils

Transition

$75/month founding rate

For women making a significant professional change.

Changing roles or industries. Returning after time away. Leaving an established path. Changing how or where you work.

Visibility & Momentum

$100/month founding rate

For women thinking seriously about what comes next without necessarily changing direction.

Greater visibility. A larger remit. More influence. Or working out the next step when progression is no longer obvious.

Recalibration

$100/month founding rate

For women reconsidering the shape of their professional life.

Changing the role work plays. Reconsidering priorities. Questioning whether the version of success you were pursuing still fits.

Founding member experience

One Council. The same room each month.

  • One 90-minute Council session each month.
  • Six women in each Council.
  • The same Council each month.
  • Neutral facilitation.
  • Pre- and post-session reflection.
  • Three-month initial commitment.
  • Founding rate locked while membership is continuous.

The pilot

Edition One is the first live test of the model.

Founding members are joining something we expect to learn from and improve.

We will ask for feedback and examine what works, what does not, and what should change.

The basic proposition stays simple: six women helping one another think through professional decisions that are still in play.

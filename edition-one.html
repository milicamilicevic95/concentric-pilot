This is a pilot

Edition One is the first live test of the model.

Concentric began with research. Edition One tests what happens when the room exists in practice.

We will examine what makes placement work, whether difference improves the conversation, what role facilitation should play, and what changes because the Council existed.

Founding members are joining a live model we expect to learn from and improve.

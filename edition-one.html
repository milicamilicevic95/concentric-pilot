The Councils

Placed around what you are navigating.

The names give the conversation a centre of gravity. Placement is still based on the person, the decision and the composition of the room.

For women making a significant professional change.

  • Changing roles or industries
  • Redundancy or an unexpected transition
  • Returning after time away
  • Leaving an established career path

For women thinking seriously about what comes next while continuing to build from where they are.

  • Working toward a larger role
  • Increasing professional visibility
  • Taking on more influence or responsibility
  • Feeling stuck despite performing well

For women reconsidering the shape of their professional life.

  • Returning after burnout
  • Recovering from an unsustainable role
  • Changing the role work plays in your life
  • Reconsidering priorities

The founding experience

A small pilot by design.

Edition One is where Concentric tests the model in practice: three deliberately composed Councils, with a three-month initial commitment.

Monthly Council

One structured 90-minute online session with the same six women.

Neutral facilitation

Structure and timekeeping without coaching or directing the answer.

Founding rate

$75/month across all three Councils, locked while membership remains continuous.

Placement, not open enrollment

We build the room before we fill the seat.

Application is followed by a Placement Conversation. We use that conversation to understand what you are navigating and whether there is a useful Council fit.

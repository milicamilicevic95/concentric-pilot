The three Councils

Transition

$75/month

For women making a significant professional change.

This may include:

  • Changing roles or industries.
  • Redundancy.
  • Returning after time away.
  • Leaving an established career path.
  • Changing how or where you work.
  • Starting a new professional chapter.

Visibility & Momentum

$75/month

For women who are not necessarily changing direction but are thinking seriously about what comes next.

This may include:

  • Working toward a larger role.
  • Increasing professional visibility.
  • Taking on more influence or responsibility.
  • Feeling stuck despite performing well.
  • Working out the next step when progression is no longer obvious.

Recalibration

$75/month

For women reconsidering the shape of their professional life.

This may include:

  • Returning after burnout.
  • Recovering from an unsustainable role.
  • Changing the role work plays in your life.
  • Reconsidering priorities.
  • Questioning whether the version of success you were pursuing still fits.

What members receive

The founding member experience.

  • One 90-minute Council session each month.
  • A Council of six women.
  • The same Council each month.
  • Neutral facilitation.
  • Pre- and post-session reflection.
  • Access to the founding member experience as the model develops.
  • Three-month initial commitment.
  • Founding rates remain locked while membership is continuous.

This is a pilot

Edition One is the first live test of the Concentric model.

That means founding members are joining something we expect to learn from and improve.

We will ask for feedback.

We will examine what works and what does not.

And we may change parts of the experience as we learn.

What will not change is the basic proposition:

Six women helping one another think through professional decisions that are still in play.

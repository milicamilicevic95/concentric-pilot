The Evidence Base

What we know. What we are testing.

Concentric began with research. Edition One is the first live test of the model.

67women across the United States

The first US study

Our first US study asked 67 women about the professional challenges they were navigating, the support available to them and what they felt was missing.

Across the responses, four themes emerged.

Questioning whether the current professional path was still the right one.
Managing professional difficulty largely alone.
Lacking some of the relationships, introductions, advocacy or opportunities needed despite having professional contacts.
Reconsidering work after burnout, depletion or an unsustainable role.
Read the research report

The question underneath

Can someone be professionally well-connected and still lack the right people for the conversations that matter?

Concentric is testing that question.

Edition One is not proof that the model works. It is the founding pilot through which we are learning what useful placement, composition, continuity and facilitation look like in practice.

What Edition One is testing

Does anything change because the Council existed?

  • Does a decision become clearer?
  • Does an assumption change?
  • Does a new option appear?
  • Does a plan survive scrutiny?
  • Is an action taken — or consciously declined?

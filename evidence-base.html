A note on the evidence

The first study gives us a question to test — not proof that Councils are the answer.

The first study involved 67 self-selected respondents in the United States. It is not a representative sample of all professional women.

Concentric is using Edition One to test the model in practice: what makes placement work, whether difference between members improves the conversation, what role facilitation should play, whether continuity matters, and what changes after the conversation.

What we will look for

Movement, not a predetermined outcome.

  • A decision becomes clearer.
  • An assumption changes.
  • A previously unseen option appears.
  • A plan becomes stronger under scrutiny.
  • An action is taken — or consciously declined.
  • A member realizes she needs more information before deciding.

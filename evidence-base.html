The Evidence Base

Concentric began with a question, not a product.

Our first US study asked 67 women what they were navigating professionally, what support they had and what they felt was missing.

67respondents · United States

Four recurring themes

Direction uncertaintyQuestions about whether the current path still fits — and what might come next.
Professional isolationHaving people around you is not the same as having the right room for an unresolved professional question.
Access gapsWomen described gaps in access to opportunities, introductions and rooms.
Professional recalibrationReconsidering priorities, sustainability and the shape professional life should take.

What the research can — and cannot — say

Directional evidence, not a representative survey.

The study was conducted in the United States with a self-selected sample. The findings are useful for identifying recurring experiences and questions; they should not be treated as representative of all professional women.

Concentric is now testing whether deliberately composed peer Councils are useful in response to what we heard.

The Council

A small room built around the question.

Six professionally established women meet online for 90 minutes each month. Members bring professional decisions, questions and tensions while they are still in play.

Apply for placement

What happens in the room

The other five women are not there to tell you what to do.

They listen closely enough to understand the decision, then help you examine it from more than one angle.

  • Ask the question you have not asked yourself.
  • Challenge an assumption you are treating as fixed.
  • Introduce a perspective you would not usually encounter.
  • Share experience where it is actually relevant.
  • Stress-test the thinking before you act.

You make your own decision.

The point is not consensus. It is better thinking.

The format

Designed for depth, not scale.

01

Six members

Enough perspectives without turning the conversation into a crowd.

02

The same women

The Council develops context over time instead of starting from zero each month.

03

Neutral facilitation

The facilitator manages structure, time and participation. She does not coach the group.

04

Monthly, online

One structured 90-minute conversation each month.

Composition

Shared context. Useful difference.

A Council is not six women with identical careers or identical problems.

Concentric deliberately composes each room around what its members are navigating — with enough shared context to understand one another and enough difference to bring useful perspective.

That is why placement happens through an application and Placement Conversation rather than open enrollment.

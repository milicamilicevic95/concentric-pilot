The conversation

Bring the question while it is still open.

  • Ask something you have not considered.
  • Challenge an assumption.
  • Share relevant experience.
  • Point out a risk or option you have overlooked.
  • Offer another way of looking at the problem.
  • Stress-test a plan before you act on it.

The Council does not vote on your decision. You make it.

What you can bring

It does not need to be dramatic.

  • Should I take this role?
  • Am I solving the wrong problem?
  • Do I actually want the promotion I have been working toward?
  • How should I approach this conversation?
  • Is this plan realistic?
  • Should I leave? Should I stay?
  • What am I missing?
  • I know something needs to change. I am not yet sure what.

What it is not

Not networking. Not coaching.

You are not meeting a new group every month or collecting contacts. There is no coach giving you the answer or setting your goals.

Concentric is a deliberately composed peer Council: experienced women bringing questions, perspective and challenge to one another’s professional decisions.

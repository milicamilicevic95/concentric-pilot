Five other minds on the question.

The conversation

What happens in the room.

Members bring what is currently occupying their professional thinking.

What you can bring

It does not need to be dramatic.

  • Should I take this role?
  • Am I solving the wrong problem?
  • Should I leave — or stay?
  • What am I missing?
  • I know something needs to change. I am not yet sure what.

The structure

Small enough to know the room. Different enough to make it useful.

Each Council has six women. Members return to the same Council each month, so the room develops context over time.

Every meeting has a neutral facilitator. Her role is structure, time and participation — not coaching members or directing them toward an answer.

Members agree to clear expectations around confidentiality and participation.

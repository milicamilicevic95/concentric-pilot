The Council

Six women. One deliberately composed Council.

Each Council is composed around what its members are navigating — with enough shared context to understand one another and enough difference to bring useful perspective.

The format

90 minutes a month.

Members bring decisions, questions and tensions while they are still in play.

The other five women ask questions, challenge assumptions, introduce different perspectives and help stress-test the thinking.

The point is not consensus. It is better thinking.

The conversation

Ask questions

The other women help surface what may be underneath the decision, not simply react to the version first presented.

What you can bring

Thinking that is still unfinished.

  • A role you are considering.
  • A career change you are unsure about.
  • A plan you want tested.
  • A difficult situation at work.
  • A decision between several reasonable options.
  • A question about what comes next.
  • Something you cannot name yet.

Bring the decision while it is still in play.
